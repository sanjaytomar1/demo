Your Accommodation

Radisson Blu Waterfront Hotel, Jersey

Located right on Rue de L'étau in St. Helier, your hotel overlooks St. Aubin’s Bay and Elizabeth Castle. Enjoy spacious family rooms, an indoor swimming pool for evening splashes with the kids, and an ideal central launching pad for all island adventures.

View Hotel Map

Island Attractions & Proximity from Hotel

Because the Radisson Blu is centrally positioned in St. Helier, no drive to any attraction on the island exceeds 20–25 minutes!

Must-Visit Attractions

  • Jersey Zoo: 32-acre sanctuary (15 mins from hotel). Great for gorillas & playgrounds.
  • Mont Orgueil Castle: Medieval fortress in Gorey (20 mins east). Secret rooms & dress-up.
  • Elizabeth Castle: Directly visible from your hotel! Reached via the amphibious Duck Boat.
  • aMaizin! Adventure Park: Go-karts, toboggans, and massive indoor play (15 mins west).
  • St Brelade's Bay: Safest golden sands & paddling for kids (12 mins west).
  • Corbière Lighthouse: Thrilling tidal causeway walk on the south-west tip.

Highly Recommended

  • Maritime Museum: Walking distance from hotel in St. Helier. Fully hands-on water play.
  • Plémont Bay & Waterfall: Dramatic caves & beach waterfall on the north coast (Low tide only).
  • Val de la Mare: Peaceful inland nature stroll to spot red squirrels.
  • St Ouen's Bay: Vast 5-mile beach on the west coast for sunset dining.

Day 1: Arrival & Radisson Blu Check-in

Thursday 20th • Waterfront Hotel, St Brelade's & Sunsets

12:30 PM

Check-in at Radisson Blu Waterfront

Arrive in Jersey, head straight into St. Helier to check into the Radisson Blu Waterfront Hotel. Drop your bags, freshen up, and take in the magnificent views across St. Aubin’s Bay.

2:00 PM - 4:00 PM

St Aubin & St Brelade's Bay

Drive just 12 minutes west from your hotel to St Brelade's Bay for an afternoon of safe paddling, golden sands, and building sandcastles.

For Kids: Safe paddling in calm waters and the nearby "Elephant Park" playground.
5:30 PM

Corbière Causeway & Ice Cream

Head further west to the dramatic south-west tip. Walk the exposed causeway at low tide to the striking white lighthouse, then grab famous Jersey Dairy ice cream.

For Kids: The thrill of the causeway and exploring rockpools.
7:30 PM

Return & Dinner at St Ouen's

Enjoy sunset dining right on the beach at El Tico Beach Cantina before returning for a restful night at the Radisson Blu Waterfront Hotel.

Radisson Blu Stay & Island Tips

Hotel Logistics

  • Indoor Pool: Perfect for unwinding with children after a day exploring castles and zoos.

  • Car Rental: The hotel provides convenient access to St. Helier ring roads and underground parking options.

  • Walking Distance: St. Helier’s shops, restaurants, and the Maritime Museum are all an easy 10-minute walk from the lobby.

Tidal Management & Safety

  • Vast Tides: Jersey has one of the largest tidal ranges in the world (up to 12 meters / 40 feet).

  • Plémont & Corbière: Only visit Plémont beach and walk the Corbière causeway at low tide. Check daily tide tables!
